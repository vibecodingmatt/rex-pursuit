## Storm conditions (2026-09-24, local only, not pushed)

Drop 1 of [the upgrade roadmap](ROADMAP.md). Menu and Pause have a **Conditions: Clear | Storm** picker stored in `localStorage` (`rex-pursuit-conditions`). Clear must stay visually and behaviourally identical; gameplay rules do not change in the storm.

- `src/chase/weather.js` owns one smoothed storm value. Rain is a single instanced draw of camera-relative streaks. Each streak's length and slant come from the drop's velocity relative to the Jeep (fall + road speed + gusts). It also owns instanced road splash rings, a lightning scheduler (multi-pulse flash, midpoint-displaced bolt placed mostly over the road corridor, sky flash, cool rim-light kick) and the overcast grade. `captureBase()` records each location's lighting (jungle and Visitor Center both call `baseLights()` first), and `apply()` blends the storm over it every frame. The post grade base is recorded once.
- `weather-state.js` shares `WET`, `RAIN`, `RAIN_TIME` and `WIND_GUST` uniforms with the ground (darkening, roughness, larger puddles, raindrop ripple normals), plants (gusts, darker bark, slightly glossier leaves) and the Rex hide (dorsal soaking, vertical runoff streaks, water in scale cavities; mouth excluded).
- `post.js` adds lens beads only where there is a real lens: third person, menu and victory exterior. The first-person gunner has no lens. `?fps` shows frame rate, frame time, tier, render scale and draw calls.
- Storm audio: `audio.weather(level)` drives a recorded rain bed (two detuned, offset copies of a crossfaded loop) that is silent inside the jaws. `audio.thunder(delay, nearness)` plays close or distant thunder after the flash, delayed by distance and capped at 6 s, with a low-pass for far strikes. The clips are in `public/audio/storm/` (CC0, credited in the README), and the context suspend on Pause covers them.
- **Lessons:** (1) screen-space-expanded instanced quads flip winding with the streak direction, so they need `side: DoubleSide` or every drop is culled while still issuing a draw call. (2) On wet surfaces the fake cool **rim light** behind the Rex mirrors straight into the gunner's view as a silver road and white leaves; the storm drops it to 55%. The sun is 6% in the storm, since dappled sun patches read as glare. Leaf specular stays near the approved values; relaxing it brought back the "snow" leaves.
- Verified: `build`, `test:logic`, `test:smoke`, `test:pressure` (see progress log), and desktop High frame cost unchanged within noise (8.1 ms storm vs 8.3 ms clear, headless RTX 3070). Captures are in ignored `art/review/storm/`, with `capture.cjs` and `quick.cjs` to repeat them.

## Foot articulation (2026-09-23)

The user said the Rex walked with her feet frozen in a flat pose. They asked for the toes and foot to curl or move realistically from lift-off to touchdown. Previously each foot moved as one rigid block, with a whole-foot tilt of at most about 6 degrees and a 3-degree bend that actually lifted the toes.

- `src/chase/foot-motion.js` now drives the rig's metatarsus, toe and dewclaw bones from step-cycle keys; `locomotion.js` feeds it the phase, duty, walk/run blend and fade. In stance the metatarsus leans back at touchdown and rolls forward over the balls of the toes. The ball of the foot then lifts, and the toes peel off from the base outward, with the claw tip leaving last. In the air the toes curl and draw together, then straighten and fan out to land flat. The dewclaw tucks.
- The middle claw tip is still the IK contact, so plants stay locked to the road. The ankle target now accounts for the articulated toes. The running lift is 0.36 (was 0.33), because the reach clamp no longer lifts the claw. Post-ram recoil steps blend back to the old rigid tilt. See [Foot roll and toes](../.agents/skills/rex-pursuit-maintainer/references/animation-and-rendering.md#foot-roll-and-toes) for the invariants and the failure modes found while tuning.
- No asset re-export. The death fall starts from whatever pose the toes hold and relaxes them with the rest of the limbs.
- Passed `test:gait`, `test:motion`, `test:defeat`, `test:cinematic`, `test:treeline`, `test:smoke`, `npm test`, `test:logic` and `build`. Knee and hip speeds in pursuit, charge and the ambush turn are below the previous gait's at 30/60/144 Hz; at 144 Hz the ambush turn went from knee 14.01 and hip 16.75 to 13.28 and 13.92. Post-spin claw vertical speed and height are lower. The recoil knee peak is 15.08 against the previous 14.85, within the 16 limit. Before/after close-ups (walk and run, side and front) and menu, third-person and phone crops were inspected. The review captures and scripts are ignored under `art/review/feet-*` and `art/review/feetgame-*`.
- Release gate: every other browser suite, `build`, `test:build`, `test:release` and `test:pages` also passed. `test:pressure` passed in seven of eight views. Its compact-phone view is intermittent on both the previous and the new gait (one failure in three runs each), independent of this change; see the [verification reference](../.agents/skills/rex-pursuit-maintainer/references/verification-and-release.md#focused-checks).

## Visual overhaul (2026-09-23)

The user asked for a studio-grade look, with a stunning Rex that stays playable on common devices, and gave creative latitude. They then asked specifically that the Rex not look washed out or too shiny, and that the inside of the mouth look normal and high fidelity when she roars. Gameplay rules, timings, rig motion, cinematics and the throat interior are unchanged.

- **Frame pipeline** (`src/chase/post.js`): the frame loop renders into an MSAA HalfFloat target, then ray-marched sun scattering (canopy mask + real shadow map), a 13-tap/tent dual-filter bloom, and a final pass (ACES, grade, vignette, grain, mild chromatic edge, sharpen under dynamic resolution). Only `renderFrame()` routes through it; direct `renderer.render()` calls (treeline masks, Water reflections, swallow composite) still work. The swallow composite draws after it to the default framebuffer. Without float render targets the game falls back to direct rendering.
- **Atmosphere** (`src/chase/atmosphere.js`): procedural sky dome with drifting clouds; a PMREM environment built from a sky + forest-band dome (replaces `RoomEnvironment`); height-attenuated fog with a warm sun lobe installed into `ShaderChunk` before any material compiles. `createCanopy()` builds one tileable leaf-gap mask used by an invisible shadow-casting plane (dappled light on everything), the volumetric shafts and the sunbeam motes, so shafts line up with ground dapples. **three filters shadow casters by the main camera's layers**, so the caster stays on layer 0 with `colorWrite:false` rather than on a separate layer.
- **Lights**: key sun from `SUN_DIRECTION`, above and behind the Jeep's right side, so the Rex is lit three-quarter on the face as she chases (the shadow camera follows the road window). There is also a dim cool rim from behind the animal, a soft fill from the Jeep side and a green/earth hemisphere. **The user found the first overhaul pass hard to see**: with the sun behind her she was a backlit silhouette against bright haze. Do not move the sun behind the Rex. The canopy caster is offset along the sun direction so the open road corridor in the mask lands on the road; post and motes subtract the same `canopy.offset`. Direct light she loses to cast shadow is partly restored on her hide (`(1-sh)*.42`) so tree shade never swallows her; volumetric density (.0085) and fog (.0138) were thinned so she is not veiled. The rim is deliberately low (0.6): at 1.25 its specular sparkled on the brow's fine scale detail. The victory arrival swaps to open-sky values and disables the canopy; restart restores `jungleLighting()`.
- **Rainforest** (`src/chase/foliage.js`, `environment.js`): all procedural. Buttressed giants with lianas and card crowns, leaning edge trees that close a canopy tunnel, palms, tree ferns, ground ferns, elephant-ear/banana leaves, lumpy mossy rocks, fallen logs and clumped instanced grass on a graded ground mesh (road ruts, verge, leaf litter, wet patches). A shared plant shader adds wind sway, leaf flutter, back-lit translucency and moss. Six unique 28 m layouts are merged per material; optional planting is shuffled so a draw range thins it by tier. The **concealment belt (15-29 m each side, four rows) is never thinned**. Its bushes have a ground-level leafy skirt and, for the larger sizes, a taller crown. Without the skirt her lower legs showed under the canopies at the frame edge in first person (about 2-3% exposure, intermittent with wind and gait phase), failing `test:treeline`'s 2% limit. `art/review/treeline-diag.cjs` reports exposed pixels and what the head ray passes through. Ground noise is periodic over one chunk; keep `28*k` whole when changing its frequencies or seams appear.
- **Rex hide** (`src/chase/rex-skin.js`, composed through `ImpactDamage.install(material, finish)` so wounds stay on top): a light regrade that keeps the authored orange-brown saturation (92%; heavier desaturation read as grey), darker dorsal bands, paler belly, triplanar Voronoi scales as screen-space bump with specular anti-aliasing, mud on feet/shins, a warm wrap term for thick skin and a restrained sky rim. Roughness keeps the accepted `.48-.86` remap, then the dry hide has a `.62` floor outside the lips and mouth. Eyes gained a limbal ring and blinks (`gaze.blink()` on hits); teeth get stained roots; the cornea became a pure additive reflection; the ribcage breathes via `ribCage_*` bones (`Ctrl_breath` has no skin influence).
- **Mouth interior**: `rexMouthMask()` marks inward-facing surfaces between the jaws in rest space, plus a `rexGape` vertex attribute (`markGape()`: 4·w·(1-w) of jaw-bone weight) for the stretched corner membrane. Inside the mask: dark crimson flesh deepening toward the throat, faint palatal ridges, no scales/mud. Direct diffuse is occluded by `rexCavity` (more toward the throat), ambient is occluded, direct specular is damped to 10%, and the hero shadow-lift and warm wrap are excluded. The front-lit sun otherwise made the open mouth glow orange. When closed, the mask is scaled by the live jaw value (`uRexJaw`), so the lip seam reads as a dark gum line rather than a red stripe. Dry hide direct specular is scaled to 40%: sun glints on the nasal ridges read as pale streaks. The damped specular matters: the cool rim/fill lights made the flattened membrane glow lilac. Use `rexChase.rex.hide.uniforms.uRexDebug.value=1` to show the mask in red, and `art/review/capture-mouth.cjs` for held-roar views.
- **Effects** (`effects.js`, `birds.js`): HDR tracers (a bright ~8 m slug travelling at a readable 130 m/s, a faint hot trail back to the muzzle, a brief hold at impact and a minimum on-screen width so rounds read end-on from the gunner's seat; the user found the first, thinner version hard to see) with an impact spark on every hit, a fireball/smoke/sparks explosion with a point-light burst and brief screen flash, blood mist on hits, dirt/bark chips on misses, roar breath mist, footfall camera jolts (off near the bite), falling leaves, a startled flock on the opening roar and ambush crash, an HDR muzzle flash, and mud/dust grime on the Jeep.
- **Quality** (`src/chase/graphics.js`): Low/Medium/High/Ultra budgets (pixel ratio, render-scale range, MSAA, bloom levels, volumetric steps/resolution, shadow size, grass/flora/particle density). Auto picks from the GPU string and device class; a frame-time governor adjusts render scale and, on sustained strain at minimum scale, drops one tier. Menu and Pause have a picker (stored in `localStorage`, key `rex-pursuit-quality`) with a readout.
- **Front end** (`index.html`, `src/chase-premium.css`): boot screen with real load progress, a staged title reveal, a slow menu camera drift, glass HUD panels and a segmented boss bar. Test-facing ids, touch-target sizes and layouts are unchanged; the controls tip hides on desktops under 861 px tall to make room for the picker.
- **Gun aim**: the gun turns toward the true world point under the reticle each frame (`aimPoint()` in `chase.js`: bone-attached proxy spheres from `rex.aimHit()`, else the ground, else 80 m), and misses end at that same point. It previously aimed at a fixed depth along the camera ray while rounds flew to the real impact, so tracers left at an angle to the barrel, obviously so in third person. Yaw/pitch damping went from 15/14 to 26. The measured barrel-vs-shot angle is ≤0.3° in first person and ≤2° in third (`art/review/aim-check.cjs`). Tracers are discrete: every round gets a short slug and every third a brighter one with a short tail, with no continuous line back to the muzzle (the user found the first version too stream-like).
- **Foliage whiteness**: leaf cards are double-sided, and card clusters use normals radiating from the bush centre. three's back-face flip turned those away from the viewer, maxing Fresnel/GGX so leaves read as snow. `plant()` undoes the flip for `volume` materials, keeps leaf normals facing the viewer and damps leaf specular. Chromatic aberration was reduced to .0016.
- **Jeep wheels** now spin forward: `rotation.x` decreases so the contact patch travels +Z with the road (it previously spun backward, which the user noticed).
- **Footfall stomp** is a vertical-only damped spring on the camera (`stompOffset`), not random `shake`. Random lateral shake moved the first-person/phone framing enough to push challenge targets under the HUD in `test:pressure`.
- **Timestep fix**: `frame()` clamps negative deltas. A rAF timestamp earlier than the clock sampled after start-up work made damped FOV blends diverge (FOV around -1e11) and broke first-person phone framing; this was the cause of the arcade/pressure layout failures during this work.
- Relative cost on this RTX 3070 laptop, headless Chrome, 1600×900 pursuit (`art/review/perf-tiers.cjs`): roughly 4-5 ms/frame across tiers with ~290-330 draw calls. That is not a physical-phone measurement; the governor and Low tier exist for weaker devices.

## Product direction

The concept evolved from a stationary encounter into a first-person mounted-gun chase, with a third-person camera showing the park-themed Jeep and its left-seat driver. Keep the cinematic sense of scale, readable arcade targets, and believable creature movement. The user notices small problems in feet, knees, hands, eye focus, jaw timing, and close-up materials.

The main accepted features are:

- A stopped Jeep opening: the Rex breaks through the right-hand jungle, roars, and follows as the vehicle accelerates. Moving roars retain locomotion; audible Rex vocals drive the jaw from their actual audio envelope.
- A 90-second active pursuit deadline with ordered targets, increasing pressure, and branches broken by physical Rex contact before they fly toward the Jeep. No special headshot text or color.
- A midpoint escape feint into the left jungle and a close return from that same side. Concealment comes from foliage along a continuous route, not hiding or teleporting the model. The road verge stays relatively open and vegetation grows denser farther away.
- Persistent bullet/explosion damage, including the face. Skin, wounds, tongue, and eyes have distinct finishes.
- An animated gun feed, ejected cases and links, articulated reload hands, a left-hand steering wheel, and a safari ranger driver visible in third person.
- Touch aiming above the thumb, independent aim/fire pointers, accessible reload/grenade controls, and suppression of selection and long-press callouts.
- A flexible, weighted Rex death fall on victory. Player defeat instead forces first person, knocks the gun away, spins the Jeep once, and ends with the Rex walking up and swallowing the player.
