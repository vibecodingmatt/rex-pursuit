    How to play

    ShootAim with the mouse, arrow keys, a stick or your finger; hold to fire. Head shots on the amber marks break a boss's attack.
    GrenadesRight-click, G, LB or the GRENADE button lobs one at the sight. Three to start, one more each stage and from repair crates.
    OverdriveKills and crates charge it; E, Y or the button slows the world for five seconds.
    CratesGreen crates repair the Jeep. Orange: explosive rounds. Blue: spread shot. Violet: piercing rounds. Barrels explode.
    UpgradesAfter each stage, shoot one of three cards (or press 1 2 3). They last the run.
    BonusesAccuracy and NO DAMAGE pay out at every clear; chain kills for up to x5. A golden compy crosses once a stage: 5,000.
    ModesThe Extended Cut, the '94 Circuit, a DAILY RUN on today's seed, a BOSS RUSH, PRACTICE for stages you have reached, and Expert for 1.5x score.
    Two playersPlug in a second gamepad and press Start to take the other gun.

        RESEARCH / REMIX / REIMAGINE

        A love letter
        to the moving seat.

        Sega AM3’s 1994 Jurassic Park put you in a bucking vehicle with joystick aiming and automatic tranquilizer fire. The journey was gloriously improbable: charging herds, giant sauropods, a volcanic cave, a falling bridge, flooded jungle and a final Rex confrontation.

        Lost Circuit remixes that structure into a new single-player ride. The ’94 Circuit follows the classic arc. The Extended Cut detours through a Jurassic World-inspired lagoon and a Fallen Kingdom-inspired conservatory, with Indominus, Mosasaurus and Indoraptor encounters.

        Hold mouse or touch to fire. Arrow keys aim; Space fires. Shoot amber head markers to interrupt boss attacks. Shoot green supplies to repair, red canisters to clear crowds, and debris before it reaches the vehicle. Chains fill Overdrive: press E or its button for five seconds of slower danger and faster fire. Esc pauses. Two continues are available; continued runs have their own records.

        An all-new 3D expedition with animated dinosaurs, cinematic boss artwork, original architectural textures, and a procedural score. Dinosaur calls come from the existing Rex: Pursuit sound library. The raptor model is by Animaniac888; scanned terrain assets are from Poly Haven. This is an unofficial fan concept, not a Sega or Universal release. No original arcade sprites, film frames or soundtrack are used.
